# Face Hugger - French encryption filing drafts

Prepared September 30, 2026 for Store version 1.0 (3). Nothing has been sent to ANSSI or submitted for App Review.

- `Face-Hugger-dossier-preparatoire-ANSSI.pdf`: three-page French worksheet, owner-detail checklist, and submission instructions.
- `Face-Hugger-description-technique-cryptographie.pdf`: six-page French technical attachment, including Python/TLS settings, Xet/Rust evidence, supported algorithms, limitations, and package checksum.
- `ANSSI-formulaire-officiel-XFA.pdf`: unmodified official form downloaded from ANSSI. It requires an XFA-capable viewer such as Adobe Acrobat Reader; ordinary PDF viewers may show only its compatibility placeholder. No fields or signature were inserted.

Before signing, confirm the declarant (individual/company), legal name, nationality, postal address, contact details, applicable operation/classification, required supporting records, and any technical questions still marked unverified. Complete those private details locally; they are deliberately absent from Git. The preparation documents are drafts, not approval certificates and must not be uploaded to Apple as proof of regulatory approval.

Official source: https://cyber.gouv.fr/documents/330/crypto_declaration-demande_autorisation_operations_annexe1_v2.pdf

Editable source: `docs/encryption-filing-worksheet.md` and `docs/encryption-technical-description.md`.
